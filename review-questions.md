# Review Questions — Introduction to Computer Networks

Use these questions for self-assessment, classroom discussion, or revision.

## Short-answer questions

1. What is data communication?
2. What is a node? Give two examples.
3. What is the role of a transmission medium?
4. Differentiate between LAN, MAN, and WAN.
5. What is the difference between a peer-to-peer network and a client-server network?
6. Distinguish between point-to-point and multipoint connections.
7. What is network topology?
8. State one advantage and one limitation of bus topology.
9. Why is the central device important in a star topology?
10. What is near-end crosstalk?
11. Give two benefits of optical fibre.
12. How does a switch differ from a hub?
13. What is the primary role of a router?
14. What are the three key elements of a protocol?
15. Is the OSI model a protocol? Explain briefly.

## Application and comparison questions

1. A small office wants a network that is easy to expand. Which topology might be considered, and what dependency should the designer remember?
2. A device must forward packets between two different networks. Which networking device is appropriate? Explain.
3. Compare UTP and STP in terms of shielding, installation, and cost.
4. Compare the responsibilities of the Physical, Data Link, and Network layers.
5. Explain the difference between node-to-node delivery and process-to-process delivery.
6. Why are both logical addressing and routing important at the Network layer?

## Diagram practice

1. Draw bus, star, ring, mesh, and tree/hybrid topologies.
2. Draw the seven OSI layers in the correct order.
3. Sketch a simple client-server network and a peer-to-peer network.
4. Draw a basic network showing a LAN connected to another network through a router.

## Reflection

Which concept in this unit do you find most difficult, and what diagram or real-world example would make it easier to understand?
